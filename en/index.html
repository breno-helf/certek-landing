About us

Excellence that builds trust

Certek Construtora Ltda is a Brazilian general contractor founded in 2012 and headquartered in Itaim Bibi, São Paulo (SP). It delivers industrial, commercial, logistics, residential, healthcare and institutional projects on a turnkey basis — from detailed design to handover — including projects certified under the U.S. Green Building Council's LEED rating system.

Working across the industrial, logistics, commercial, healthcare, residential and institutional sectors, we offer complete construction solutions grounded in excellence, safety and sustainability, with a rigorous commitment to schedule and cost and full transparency at every stage.

Beyond putting up solid structures, we build lasting client relationships — grounded in transparency, efficiency and consistently high-quality delivery.

What we do

From detailed design to turnkey

We deliver construction projects at every phase: detailed design, strategic planning and full cost estimating, through to turnkey handover. We coordinate multidisciplinary teams and manage subcontractors, holding tight control of cost, schedule and quality.

  1. Detailed design

    Full design development and coordination across every discipline.

  2. Planning and budget

    Schedule and full cost plan closed before the first excavation.

  3. Construction and site management

    Coordination of multidisciplinary teams and management of subcontractors.

  4. Turnkey handover

    Facilities ready to operate, matched to each client's requirements.

Industrial

Manufacturing plants, expansions and retrofits for the chemical, pharmaceutical and pulp-and-paper industries.

Commercial

Stores, offices and retail centers, built without shutting the operation down.

Warehouses, distribution centers and terminals for logistics operators.

Frequently asked questions

What people usually ask us

Which sectors does Certek build in?

Industrial, commercial, logistics, residential, healthcare and institutional — the six sectors the company has delivered projects in since 2012.

What does turnkey delivery include?

Every phase of the project: detailed design, planning and budgeting, construction with coordination of multidisciplinary teams and management of subcontractors, through to handover of a facility ready to operate.

Can Certek build while the plant keeps running?

Yes. Delivered projects include the SCA Hygiene Products plant, built without shutting the operation down. Certek also works in hospital environments and in commercial units that stay open.

Does Certek deliver LEED-certified projects?

Yes. Certek builds projects certified under the LEED rating system of the U.S. Green Building Council (USGBC).

Where does Certek operate?

The company is headquartered in Itaim Bibi, São Paulo (SP). Delivered projects include the Klabin forest products terminal in Paranaguá (PR) and Clara Resorts in Ibiúna (SP).
