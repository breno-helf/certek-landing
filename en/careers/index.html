Why Certek

What your CV gains here

Certek is led by professionals with more than 25 years of experience in construction. In practice, that means learning from people who have already delivered a great deal of it.

Clients who expect a standard

Klabin, Johnson & Johnson, L'Oréal, Bayer, Albert Einstein, Sírio-Libanês, adidas, Hines. Forty brands served since 2012 — each with its own bar to clear.

Six sectors, not one

Industrial, commercial, logistics, residential, healthcare and institutional. Nobody here spends a whole career repeating the same kind of project.

Genuinely hard construction

A manufacturing plant built without stopping production, works inside hospitals, a port terminal. This is where an engineer learns what easy jobs never teach.

Sustainability that gets audited

Projects certified to LEED, the U.S. Green Building Council rating system — externally verified, not a slogan. Plus the 20th Prêmio Master Imobiliário, in 2014.

Roles

Who Certek usually hires

Roles Certek has advertised include engineering internships, engineering technical assistants, site foremen, electricians, workplace safety technicians and administrative coordinators — in São Paulo and Campinas.

Open positions are posted on Certek's LinkedIn page. If your field is not on the list above, send your CV anyway: a new project opens new roles.

How to apply

Two routes, both official

CV by email certek@certek.com.br Attach your CV as a PDF and say, in two lines, the role you are after and where you can work. The subject line is filled in for you.
LinkedIn Certek's LinkedIn page This is where open positions are announced. Worth following the page to catch the next one.

Beware of fake job ads

Certek has publicly warned about job adverts posted in its name by third parties. The only official channels are the email address certek@certek.com.br and Certek's LinkedIn page. If you are unsure about an advert or a message, check with one of those two before sending any document.
